ClearSight

Private, on-device content blur

Before Protection starts

To cover unwanted content, ClearSight reads the current page URL, media URLs and pixels, nearby link destinations, and limited page labels and navigation text, including in eligible embedded frames. It checks them locally while Protection is on. This page information and results are not sent to ClearSight or saved in extension storage. Your settings are saved in Chrome's local extension storage.

If a site blocks direct media access, ClearSight may request eligible media from its source using your browser session. That site receives the request.

ClearSight can miss unsafe content or cover safe content by mistake. Model scores are not accuracy guarantees.

Privacy & website access

ClearSight needs access to websites you visit to read page URLs, media sources and pixels, and nearby link destinations for images, thumbnails, GIFs, CSS backgrounds, and videos, including eligible embedded frames. Scanning runs while Protection is on. These values and classifier results are processed in memory and are not sent to ClearSight or saved in extension storage.

The bundled NSFWJS and NudeNet models analyze media on this device. For protected cross-origin media, the extension may request it from its source website using your browser session; that site receives the normal request.

Optional tab capture is only used after you enable it and grant Chrome permission. Captured frames and audio stay local; audio is passed through to keep playback working.
